Research

Designing AI literacy for elementary students.

An HCI research focus on helping Class 1-5 students understand, evaluate, and use AI responsibly through child-friendly learning experiences.

Human-Computer Interaction AI Education Elementary Learners

Research Topic

AI Literacy for Elementary Students

This study explores how stories, games, visuals, and hands-on activities can make AI concepts understandable and meaningful for young learners in Bangladeshi classrooms.

Explore Students' and teachers' understanding of AI, current tool use, misconceptions, and ethical concerns.
Co-design Develop an activity-first learning tool with games, stories, visual materials, and teacher guidance.
Evaluate Assess usability, engagement, and effectiveness in improving AI awareness and responsible use.

Design priorities: age-appropriate language, safety and ethics, accessibility, affordability, and low-resource classroom use.
